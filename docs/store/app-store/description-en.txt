Missing your friends? Invite them to a game of buraco.

Buraco Lounge brings the Brazilian card game to your phone and tablet. Play with friends, or practise against bots without creating an account.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Live: everyone plays now, with a turn clock. If someone drops, a bot holds their seat until they're back.
• At your pace: play when you can. We let you know when it's your turn.
• 2 players, or 4 in pairs with your partner across the table.

THE RULES YOU PLAY BY
• Aberto: take the discard pile at any time.
• Fechado: take the pile only by using its top card in a meld right away.
• STBL: no lavadeiras, and you go out only with a clean canastra.
• Clean, dirty and royal canastras, the morto, and lavadeiras you can turn off.
• You choose the points to win.

INVITE YOUR FRIENDS
Create a private table and share its code. Only people with the code can join. Seats without a friend become bots when the game starts.

BOTS AT THREE LEVELS
Relaxed, standard or sharp. Practise whenever you like, no account needed, with hints if you want them.

A WORD AT THE TABLE
Short messages and quick phrases between hands. Mute a player, report a message or block someone, and the host can turn chat off.

COMFORTABLE TO PLAY
Large, clear cards, a readable font, dark mode, portrait or landscape, and layouts made for tablets. In Portuguese, English and Danish.

NO ADS, NO RUSH
No ads and no tracking. If you like, you can make an optional contribution in Settings to help pay for the servers that keep the tables running. It unlocks nothing: everyone plays the same game.

Sign in with Apple or Google to play with friends.
